Component Overview

  • Flask Application: Orchestrates requests, manages prompts, and interfaces with GCP services.
  • Cloud TTS: Handles standard and neural voice synthesis (Chirp, Journey, Neural2).
  • Gemini 2.5 Flash: Provides advanced multimodal TTS capabilities with rich style prompting.
  • Cloud Storage: Persists generated audio files and job history (JSON).
