TTS Generator Help

Overview

This application allows you to synthesize speech using Google Cloud Text-to-Speech (TTS) and Vertex AI Gemini TTS. You can generate audio from text or documents, manage history, and stream audio.

Usage Guide

1. Generator Tab

  • Input: Enter a "Job Name" (optional), type text, or upload a .txt/.pdf file.
  • Service: Choose between "Google Cloud TTS" (Standard/Premium voices) or "Gemini TTS" (Generative voices).
  • Voice: Filter and select your desired voice.
  • Controls: Adjust speed and volume (Chirp/Standard voices only).
  • Generate: Click "Generate Speech" to create the audio.

2. History Tab

  • View a list of all past generation jobs.
  • Play audio directly in the browser.
  • Download audio or source text.
  • Delete unwanted jobs from storage.

Troubleshooting

If you encounter issues, ensure your project has the required APIs enabled (Text-to-Speech, Vertex AI, Cloud Storage) and that your user/service account has the necessary permissions.
